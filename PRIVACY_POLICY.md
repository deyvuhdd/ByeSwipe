# ByeSwipe Privacy Policy

*Last updated: February 2025*

ByeSwipe ("we," "our," or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your data.

## Data Collection

ByeSwipe **does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data** to our servers or third parties. All processing happens locally on your device.

## Photo Library Access

The app requests access to your photo library to:

- Display photos and videos for browsing
- Allow you to delete photos (moved to Photos "Recently Deleted")
- Mark favorites from your library

Your photos and videos never leave your device. We do not upload, analyze, or share your media.

## Local Storage

The app may store preferences and session data locally on your device (e.g., which photos you've kept) using Apple's standard storage. This data stays on your device and is not transmitted.

## Third-Party Services

ByeSwipe does not use analytics, advertising, or any third-party tracking.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact us at the support email on our App Store page.
